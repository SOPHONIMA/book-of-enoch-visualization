# 🎬 VISUALIZATION PRINCIPLES

SOPHONIMA follows several core principles:

- Follow the Book of Enoch sequentially
- Preserve chapter and verse references
- Keep the meaning of the source text central
- Transform ancient imagery into cinematic visual storytelling
- Maintain continuity across the complete journey
- Clearly distinguish cinematic interpretation from the ancient text itself

---

# 📜 PRIMARY TEXT

The visualization project primarily follows the **R. H. Charles English translation of 1 Enoch (1917).**

Chapter and verse references are preserved so viewers can identify the passage behind each visualization.

Where the ancient text does not provide a complete physical description, visual elements may use cinematic interpretation rather than claiming that interpretation as part of the original text.

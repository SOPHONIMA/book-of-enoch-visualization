# 🐑 THE BOOK OF DREAM VISIONS

## 1 Enoch — Chapters 83–90

This section documents the SOPHONIMA visual journey through the Book of Dream Visions.

These chapters contain Enoch's symbolic dreams, including the extraordinary vision commonly known as the **Animal Apocalypse**, where human history is represented through animals, transformations, heavenly intervention, judgment, and restoration.

SOPHONIMA follows the text sequentially — chapter by chapter and verse by verse.

---

## ✨ THE JOURNEY

### 🌊 Chapters 83–84
**The First Dream Vision**

Enoch receives a vision of destruction and responds with prayer.

### 🐂 Chapters 85–90
**The Animal Apocalypse**

A vast symbolic vision of history unfolds through animals and extraordinary heavenly imagery.

Human beings and peoples appear symbolically as bulls, sheep, wild animals, birds, and other creatures as the vision progresses.

## 🎬 VISUALIZATION APPROACH

The animals and transformations in these chapters belong to the symbolic language of Enoch's dream vision.

SOPHONIMA aims to:

- Preserve the chapter and verse references
- Keep the symbolic animals central to the visualization
- Maintain continuity throughout the Animal Apocalypse
- Distinguish the ancient text from cinematic interpretation
- Avoid presenting added visual details as though they were explicitly stated in 1 Enoch

---

## 📜 PRIMARY TEXT

The project primarily follows the **R. H. Charles English translation of 1 Enoch (1917).**

The ancient text remains the foundation.

The cinematic imagery is the visual interpretation.
